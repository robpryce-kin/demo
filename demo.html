Biomechanics Demo

Kinesiology is the study of human movement. It includes several scientific disciplines such as anatomy, physiology, psychology, motor development, and biomechanics. In biomechanics we use tools like motion sensors and cameras to measure how the body moves. This information is used by scientists, coaches, and health professionals.
